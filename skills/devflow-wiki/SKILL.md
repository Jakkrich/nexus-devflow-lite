# DevFlow Wiki

Capture durable knowledge after it is verified.

Before working, read `../_shared/references/devflow-conventions.md` when available.

## Process

1. Confirm the knowledge is reusable.
2. Extract the lesson, context, and evidence.
3. Create `wiki_note.md` from `references/templates/wiki_note.template.md`.
